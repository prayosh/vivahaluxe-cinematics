The Masters

A dual-architect collective of elite fashion directors and award-winning photojournalists freezing heritage union sequences.

CO-FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Ranveer Singh Vardhan — Chief Editorial Director

Formally trained in visual direction at the prestigious Film and TV Academy of the Performing Arts in Prague, Ranveer brings 12+ years of premium European high-fashion photography directly into high-end Indian destination events.

His visual signature relies heavily on high contrast, complex off-centre geometry, and studio strobe alignment during outdoor sequences. To Ranveer, a wedding is not a traditional log; it is a live cinematic set where every frame must be worthy of an editorial gallery print.

CO-FOUNDER & MASTER OF CANDIDS

Priya Sharma Kapur — Master of Candids

An award-winning photojournalist, Priya spent over a decade documenting complex sociological stories and ritual structures for international editorial prints. She transitioned into luxury wedding photojournalism to focus on the raw, unfiltered emotional theater of Indian families.

Priya's specialty is invisibility. She moves through ceremonies without flash or instruction, capturing fleeting tears, rapid glances, shared laughter, and quiet hands. She seeks to frame honest stories that remain raw long after the glamour settles.

EXPERTISE Unobtrusive Documentaries
